BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)
Project Name

AI Dental Receptionist Platform for Canadian Clinics

1. Executive Summary
1.1 Project Overview

The AI Dental Receptionist Platform is a voice-based AI system designed to automate inbound phone call handling for dental clinics in Canada. The platform will function as a 24/7 virtual receptionist capable of answering patient calls, booking appointments, rescheduling visits, handling cancellations, responding to frequently asked questions, and escalating emergency situations when necessary.

The system will primarily target small to medium-sized dental clinics that experience high call volumes, receptionist overload, missed appointments, and after-hours communication gaps.

The platform will support:

English and French conversations
Real-time voice interaction
Google Calendar integration
Internal scheduling system
Emergency escalation workflows
Appointment management
Administrative dashboard
Call transcripts and analytics

The initial product scope will focus only on:

inbound voice calls
dental clinic workflows
Google Calendar support
proprietary internal scheduler

The system will not initially support:

outbound AI calling
SMS/chat-based communication
medical diagnosis
insurance claim processing
third-party dental software integrations
1.2 Business Objective

The primary objective of the platform is to reduce missed patient calls and automate repetitive receptionist tasks while improving appointment conversion rates and operational efficiency for dental clinics.

The platform aims to:

reduce receptionist workload
increase appointment booking efficiency
provide after-hours call coverage
reduce patient wait times
improve clinic responsiveness
support bilingual patient communication
create a scalable AI receptionist service model
1.3 Business Problem Statement

Dental clinics frequently experience operational inefficiencies caused by:

unanswered phone calls
overloaded reception staff
missed appointment opportunities
inconsistent after-hours communication
repetitive FAQ handling
scheduling conflicts
language barriers in bilingual regions

Many clinics lose potential revenue due to missed calls during:

lunch breaks
peak operational hours
after-hours periods
staff shortages

Manual receptionist operations also increase labor costs and reduce scalability.

The proposed AI system aims to address these operational gaps through real-time conversational automation and intelligent scheduling workflows.

1.4 Expected Business Outcomes

Expected outcomes include:

increased appointment booking rates
reduced missed call percentage
reduced receptionist workload
improved patient response time
improved operational efficiency
increased after-hours booking capability
scalable multi-clinic deployment capability

# 6. Call Workflow Scenarios

# 6.1 New Patient Appointment Booking

## Scenario Description

A new patient calls the clinic to book a dental appointment for the first time.

---

## Workflow

### Step 1

System answers inbound call.

---

### Step 2

AI greets caller professionally.

Example:

> “Thank you for calling Maple Dental Clinic. How may I assist you today?”

---

### Step 3

Caller requests appointment booking.

Example:

> “I’d like to book a cleaning appointment.”

---

### Step 4

AI identifies patient type.

Example:

> “Are you a new or existing patient?”

---

### Step 5

AI collects:

* preferred date
* preferred time
* preferred provider (optional)

---

### Step 6

System checks availability.

---

### Step 7

AI presents available time slots.

---

### Step 8

Caller selects preferred slot.

---

### Step 9

AI collects patient information:

* full name
* phone number
* email (optional)

---

### Step 10

System creates appointment.

---

### Step 11

AI confirms booking.

---

## Success Outcome

Appointment successfully created.

---

## Failure Conditions

* no available slots
* invalid patient information
* scheduling conflict
* repeated communication failure

---

## Required Backend Tools

* check_availability()
* create_patient()
* create_appointment()
* send_confirmation()

---

## Mitigation

* tool-driven architecture
* backend validation
* restricted AI permissions
* deterministic scheduling logic

---

# 15.2 Scheduling Conflict Risk

## Risk

---

## Mitigation

* centralized scheduling engine
* conflict validation
* transactional appointment creation
* source-of-truth scheduling architecture

---

## Risk

Slow AI responses may create poor conversational experience.

---

## Mitigation

* streaming voice infrastructure
* optimized prompts
* low-latency providers
* concise response generation

# 15.4 Emergency Handling Risk

## Mitigation

* rapid transfer logic

# 16. Conclusion

The AI Dental Receptionist Platform is intended to automate repetitive front-desk operations for Canadian dental clinics through real-time conversational AI and intelligent scheduling workflows.

The platform focuses on:

* operational reliability
* appointment workflow automation
* multilingual communication
* after-hours availability
* scalable deployment

The initial release intentionally limits scope to:

* inbound voice calls
* dental clinic workflows
* Google Calendar support
* internal scheduling systems

This controlled scope is designed to:

* reduce engineering complexity
* improve deployment speed
* maintain scheduling reliability
* achieve faster market validation

Future expansion opportunities include:

* third-party dental software integrations
* outbound AI communication
* advanced analytics
* multi-industry expansion

The long-term strategic objective is to establish a reliable AI-driven receptionist infrastructure capable of supporting operational automation across appointment-driven service businesses.
